Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hiring AURA GLOBAL for your remote web project.

Can I hire AURA GLOBAL if I am located outside Indonesia? ↓

Yes, absolutely. A significant portion of our clients work with us 100% remotely. We communicate efficiently across international time zones via WhatsApp, Telegram, or Email, and share staging links for regular milestone reviews.

How long does it take to deliver a completed website? ↓

Starter Bio and single-page landing pages typically take 2 to 4 business days. Multi-page business websites usually take 5 to 7 business days once your brand materials and content guidelines are received.

What payment methods do you accept for international clients? ↓

We accept payments via PayPal, Wise (formerly TransferWise), and International SWIFT Bank Wire. We work on a 50% deposit to initiate work and 50% final payment after you inspect and approve the completed staging website.

Who owns the website and source code once completed? ↓

You maintain 100% full ownership of your domain, web assets, and source code. There are no proprietary lock-ins or hidden retention clauses.

Do you provide maintenance and ongoing support? ↓

Yes, all packages include 30 days of complimentary post-launch technical warranty. Ongoing monthly maintenance and server monitoring packages are also available upon request.
